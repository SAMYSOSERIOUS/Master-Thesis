Research / educational use

This interface is designed for transparency and academic presentation

The grader presents a deterministic workflow that makes the prediction, the evidence, and the reasoning easier to inspect, compare, and discuss.

What this page demonstrates

  • How the same image is processed under multiple preprocessing pipelines.
  • How the final reliability verdict is inferred from spread and agreement.
  • How the explanatory sections are generated without relying on an LLM.

Important note

This system is not a medical device and should not be used for clinical decision-making. It is intended for research, education, and demonstration.
