# Research

Canonical collection of the thesis notebooks and supporting research assets.
All research notebooks live here to avoid duplicate directory trees.

## Folders

- `calibration`: Calibration and stability experiments for model tuning and ensemble behavior.
- `diagnostics`: Cross-dataset diagnostic analyses and comparison notebooks.
- `docs`: Reports, notes, and generated metadata.
- `Evaluation`: Prediction-level comparison and ensemble analyses.
- `experiments`: Ablations, maximum-stack runs, and helper studies.
- `mendeley`: Mendeley dataset acquisition and preparation.
- `mrkr`: MRKR download and preprocessing.
- `mt3`: Fused cascade experiments.
- `nhanes3`: NHANES III ingestion and label processing.
- `novel`: Provenance, preprocessing-robustness, and concept-bottleneck studies.
- `oai`: OAI download, preprocessing, labels, and pipeline notebooks.
- `oarsi`: OARSI sub-feature extraction and multi-task learning.
- `pipeline_v1`: Primary end-to-end training and evaluation sequence (`00` through `14`).
- `single_source`: Single-dataset baselines.
- `SOTA`: Dataset-specific benchmark comparisons.

## Top-Level Notebook

- `dataset_status.ipynb`: Dataset inventory and processing status.
